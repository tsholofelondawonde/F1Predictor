# Cookie Consent

- Explain cookie usage clearly; let users accept or reject non-essential
  cookies.
- Don't load non-essential trackers before consent is given.
- Essential cookies may bypass consent where legally permitted.
- No dark patterns; keyboard accessible; works on mobile; consent is
  changeable later.
- Compliance requirements depend on the app's actual jurisdiction and
  audience — don't assume one regime fits all.
