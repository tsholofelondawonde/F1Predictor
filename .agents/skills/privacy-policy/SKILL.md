# Privacy Policy

Cover, where applicable: what's collected, why, how it's used and stored,
third-party services, cookies/tracking, retention, user rights, contact
info, and how users can request access or deletion.

State clearly when delivering this: it's a draft, not legal advice, and
needs review by an actual lawyer before it goes live.
