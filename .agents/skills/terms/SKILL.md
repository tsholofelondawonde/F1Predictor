# Terms of Service

Cover, where applicable: acceptable use, account responsibilities,
intellectual property, service availability, limitations of liability,
termination, changes to the service, and contact info.

State clearly when delivering this: it's a draft, not legal advice, and
needs review by an actual lawyer before it goes live.
